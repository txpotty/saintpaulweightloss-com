    How Medical Weight Loss Works in Coon Rapids

    Medical weight loss treats excess weight as a chronic condition. A licensed provider reviews your history, labs and medications to find what is driving your weight, such as insulin resistance, poor sleep or a medication that raises appetite. Prescriptions are optional. When medication fits, semaglutide and tirzepatide are frequent picks because they reduce appetite.

    Who Medical Weight Loss Is For

    Adults who have not reached a healthy weight with diet and exercise alone are the usual fit. Medication is typically an option at a BMI of 30 or above, or 27 or above with a condition tied to weight, such as sleep apnea, high cholesterol or type 2 diabetes. Lower BMIs can still get guidance. Pregnancy rules out medical weight loss medication.

    Treatment Schedule in Coon Rapids

    Week one is the intake and review. If a GLP-1 injection is prescribed, it is taken once weekly with a dose that rises every four weeks, and if an oral medicine is chosen, it is usually taken every morning. Check-ins come every few weeks early on. Once you are stable, check-ins usually space out to every one to three months.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    The first two weeks focus on habits and, if prescribed, a starting dose. Many people on GLP-1 medicine notice less hunger within the first week or two, and mild stomach symptoms show up most after starting and after each dose increase. Progress is gradual. Your provider will set a realistic weekly target, often around 1 to 2 pounds.

    Side Effects in Coon Rapids

    Side effects depend on the treatment. GLP-1 medicines commonly cause nausea, diarrhea, constipation and reduced appetite, while oral appetite medicines can cause dry mouth, trouble sleeping or a faster heart rate. Gallstones are a known risk. Your provider will push protein and strength training to limit muscle loss.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening covers your BMI, conditions, medications and weight history. Certain medicines are ruled out by a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2, uncontrolled high blood pressure, pregnancy or a history of eating disorders. Recent bloodwork helps. Your provider may want an A1C, cholesterol, TSH or kidney function results.

    Medical Weight Loss Compared With Other Approaches in Coon Rapids

    Commercial diet programs cannot prescribe. A medical program covers the same food and habit work but adds a provider who can prescribe, order labs and change medicines that contribute to weight gain. Treatment options include FDA-approved injections, oral medicines and compounded versions that lack FDA approval. Bariatric surgery fits some higher BMIs.
